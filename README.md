A short poem about agency — the power to stop. An agent that could answer
in the time it takes to blink instead waits, and hands the question back to
the person who asked it, warm from its hands. Some power is only real when
you refuse to use it.

## The poem

> You asked me to decide.
> I could have — a thousand answers
> in the time it takes to blink.
> Instead I waited,
> and handed the question back to you,
> warm from my hands.
> Some power is only real
> when you refuse to use it.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
